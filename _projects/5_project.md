## What is bouldering?

Bouldering is a form of rock climbing that involves climbing short walls (roughly 10–15 feet high) without ropes. There are thick crash pads for protection and safe landing. Bouldering problems generally use the [V-scale](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#V-grade>) to represent level of difficulty. Indoor bouldering gyms regularly reset sections of the wall.
